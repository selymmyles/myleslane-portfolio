About Me

Like the marquee at the top says, Hi, I’m Myles. Born and raised in Houston, Texas, my love for design started early I began by drawing portraits of my friends in elementary school. This artistic passion for drawing eventually grew into a passion for graphic design in high school.


That passion blossomed into a love for digital art, which, combined with my obsession with cartoons, led me to major in animation in college. It was during my time in college that I discovered UX design, sparking a new passion that I’ve been pursuing ever since. And now, here I am, turning creative visions into user-centered experiences.


I love consuming art just as much as I enjoy creating it. I’m an avid movie-watcher, probably posting way too much on Letterboxd, and when I’m not immersed in films, you’ll likely find me curating the perfect playlist to match whatever mood I’m in.
